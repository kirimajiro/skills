# 03 市場の屋台（長い指示への忠実さ: 数と順序）

## Generic prompt

```text
A wooden market stall with exactly three wicker baskets in a row on the counter: the left basket holds red apples, the middle one holds green pears, and the right one holds yellow lemons. A handwritten chalkboard sign leans against the counter on the right. Behind the stall, a striped red-and-white awning.
```

- 日除けの赤白ストライプ
